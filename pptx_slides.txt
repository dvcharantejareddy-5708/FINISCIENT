=== SLIDE 3 ===
WHY FINANCE TOOLS STILL FEEL REACTIVE
Problem Statement & Understanding
FINISCIENT • SH-104
03
SH-104  |  AI-POWERED PERSONAL FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE
The gap is not more data. It is better timing.
People can see what happened. They still cannot tell what to do before the next purchase.
01
Bad timing, alert fatigue
Most apps warn you after the money's already spent. By the time the alert shows up, there's nothing left to do.
02
No personal context
A flat spending cap doesn't know you. It can't tell if you're at a store you visit every week or one you've never been to.
03
Real vs. predicted
People can't easily tell what's a confirmed transaction and what's just a guess or a goal the app made up.
CUSTOMER PROBLEM
Avoid impulse buys, track subscriptions, and reach savings goals without complex Excel sheets.
Our core understanding & approach
Shift from tracking what already happened to predicting and shaping what happens next.
1
OBSERVE
Personal baseline comparisons explain unusual spending in simple language.
2
PREDICT
Dynamic simulations show how choices delay goal ETAs or create cash-flow pressure.
3
INTERVENE
Pre-spending cues arrive while a decision can still be adjusted.
Target market  •  Students  •  Young professionals  •  UPI users on a budget
